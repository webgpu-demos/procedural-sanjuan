# People

`m01.glb`: Male_Adult_01 of the [Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox) avatar library, with its clips m_idle_neutral_01, m_walk_neutral_01, m_run_neutral_01, m_walk_fast_01 (in place) and the first frame of m_sit_chair_idle_neutral_01; converted with Blender (`tools/assets/rocketbox_to_glb.py`) to glTF: the clips retargeted onto the avatar's skeleton (each bone turned as its namesake turns in the world, baked), textures 1024 px WebP, specular maps dropped.
